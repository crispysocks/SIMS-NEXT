## 规则

一般过去时用来描述**在过去某个时间点已经完成的动作**。

- 规则动词：动词原形 + **-ed**
  - walk → walked, play → played, study → studied
- 不规则动词：需要单独记忆
  - go → went, eat → ate, see → saw

## 时间标志词

yesterday, last night, last week, in 2010, ... ago, this morning

## 常见错误

- ❌ She **go** to the store yesterday.
- ✅ She **went** to the store yesterday.

中文没有动词时态变化，所以学习者容易直接用动词原形，忘记变为过去式。

## 例句

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例句 |
|------|--------|------|
| go | went | She went to school yesterday. |
| eat | ate | They ate dinner at 7pm. |
| see | saw | I saw that movie last week. |
| take | took | He took the bus this morning. |
| write | wrote | She wrote a letter yesterday. |
