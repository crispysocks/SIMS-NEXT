## 规则

**the** 用在说话双方都知道的、特定的人或事物前面。

## 什么时候用 the？

1. **独一无二的事物**
   - the sun, the moon, the earth, the world

2. **上文已提到过**
   - I saw **a dog**. **The dog** was very big.

3. **最高级前面**
   - She is **the best** student in the class.

4. **特定语境下明确的**
   - Please close **the door**. (双方都知道是哪扇门)

5. **乐器前面**
   - She plays **the piano**. He plays **the guitar**.

6. **序数词前面**
   - **the first**, **the second**, **the last**

## 常见错误

- ❌ Sun rises in east. → ✅ **The** sun rises in **the** east.
- ❌ She is best student. → ✅ She is **the** best student.
- ❌ I play piano. → ✅ I play **the** piano.

## 对比

| 泛指 (a/an) | 特指 (the) |
|-------------|-----------|
| I saw a car. (任何一辆车) | The car was red. (刚才那辆) |
| She is a teacher. (一个老师) | She is the teacher. (那个特定的老师) |
