## 规则

有些不定代词看起来像复数，但实际上要搭配**单数动词**。

## 常见不定代词（用单数动词）

| 不定代词 | 例句 |
|----------|------|
| everyone | Everyone **wants** to succeed. |
| everybody | Everybody **is** here. |
| someone | Someone **has** the answer. |
| each | Each of the students **is** ready. |
| neither | Neither of them **was** correct. |
| nobody | Nobody **knows** the truth. |

## 特殊名词

以下名词看起来像复数，但实际上是单数：
- news: The news **is** surprising.
- mathematics: Mathematics **is** difficult.
- the United States: The United States **is** a country.

## 就近原则

当主语由 "neither...nor..." 或 "either...or..." 连接时，动词与**最近的名词**保持一致。

- Neither the teacher **nor** the students **were** happy.
- Either John **or** Mary **is** going to lead the meeting.
