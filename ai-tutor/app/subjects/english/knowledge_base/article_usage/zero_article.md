## 规则

有些情况下英语**不需要**任何冠词（a/an/the），这叫做"零冠词"。

## 什么时候不用冠词？

1. **泛指复数名词**
   - ❌ I like the dogs. → ✅ I like dogs.

2. **抽象名词（泛指）**
   - ❌ The honesty is important. → ✅ Honesty is important.

3. **交通工具（by + 交通方式）**
   - by bus, by car, by train, by plane, on foot

4. **一日三餐**
   - have breakfast, eat lunch, have dinner

5. **专有名词（大部分）**
   - China, Tom, Beijing, Monday, January

6. **体育运动和学科**
   - play football, study mathematics

## 常见错误

- ❌ I go to school by **the** bus. → ✅ I go to school by bus.
- ❌ She had **a** breakfast at 7am. → ✅ She had breakfast at 7am.
- ❌ **The** Mount Everest is tall. → ✅ Mount Everest is tall.

## 记忆技巧

当你在说**泛指概念**而非**特定个体**时 → 不用冠词。
