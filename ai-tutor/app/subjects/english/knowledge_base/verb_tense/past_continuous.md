## 规则

过去进行时用来描述**过去某个时间点正在进行的动作**。

结构：**was / were + 动词-ing**

- I / He / She / It → **was** + V-ing
- You / We / They → **were** + V-ing

## 用法

1. 过去某个时刻正在发生的动作
   - At 3pm yesterday, I **was reading** a book.

2. 一个动作正在进行时，另一个动作发生了
   - She **was walking** to school when it started raining.

3. 两个动作同时进行
   - I **was cooking** while he **was cleaning**.

## 常见错误

- ❌ She **walking** to school when it rained. (缺少 was)
- ❌ They **was playing** football. (were 不是 was)

## 例句

- She was walking to school when it started raining.
- They were playing football at 3pm yesterday.
- I was reading a book when she called.
