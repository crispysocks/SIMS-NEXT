## 规则

当主语是**复数**时，一般现在时的动词用**原形**（不加 -s）。

- The cat **sleeps** (单数) → The cats **sleep** (复数)
- A student **studies** (单数) → Students **study** (复数)

## 常见复数主语类型

1. 复数名词：dogs, books, children, people
2. 复合主语 (A and B)：Tom and Jerry **are** friends.
3. 代词：we, you, they

## 常见错误

- ❌ The dogs **barks** loudly. → ✅ The dogs **bark** loudly.
- ❌ Children **likes** to play. → ✅ Children **like** to play.
- ❌ My parents **is** at home. → ✅ My parents **are** at home.

## 特别提醒

不要在复数主语后面画蛇添足地加 -s。看到主语是复数 → 动词保持原形。
