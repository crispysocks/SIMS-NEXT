## 规则

英语中很多动词、形容词和名词有**固定的介词搭配**，不能用其他介词替换。

这种搭配没有逻辑规律，需要逐个记忆。

## 常见形容词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| good at | She is good **at** math. |
| interested in | He is interested **in** art. |
| afraid of | She is afraid **of** spiders. |
| angry with | He is angry **with** me. |
| famous for | Paris is famous **for** the Eiffel Tower. |
| responsible for | She is responsible **for** the team. |
| different from | It's different **from** what I expected. |

## 常见动词 + 介词

| 搭配 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| apologize for | He apologized **for** being late. |
| insist on | She insisted **on** paying. |
| succeed in | He succeeded **in** passing the exam. |
| depend on | It depends **on** the weather. |
| wait for | I'm waiting **for** the bus. |
| belong to | This book belongs **to** me. |

## 常见错误

- ❌ She is good **in** math. → ✅ She is good **at** math.
- ❌ I'm interested **on** learning. → ✅ I'm interested **in** learning.
- ❌ He apologized **about** being late. → ✅ He apologized **for** being late.

## 记忆技巧

把"动词+介词"当作一个整体来记，不要分开记。就像记"look at"、"listen to"这样的短语一样。
