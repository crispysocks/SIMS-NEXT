## 规则

现在完成时连接**过去和现在**，描述过去发生的动作对现在的影响或持续到现在的状态。

结构：**have / has + 过去分词 (Past Participle)**

- I / You / We / They → **have** + past participle
- He / She / It → **has** + past participle

## 用法

1. 过去开始，持续到现在
   - She **has lived** here since 2010.

2. 过去的经历（不强调具体时间）
   - I **have visited** Beijing three times.

3. 刚刚完成的动作
   - He **has just finished** his homework.

## 常见错误

- ❌ She **has went** to the store. (went → gone)
- ❌ I **have eat** breakfast. (eat → eaten)
- ❌ He **have seen** that movie. (have → has)

## 例句

- She has been studying English for three years.
- I have finished my homework.
- They have lived here since 2010.
