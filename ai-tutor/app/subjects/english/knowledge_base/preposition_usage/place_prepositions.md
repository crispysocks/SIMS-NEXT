## 规则

地点介词描述物体在空间中的位置。

## 常用地点介词

- **at** — 具体地点、地址
  - at home, at school, at the bus stop, at 123 Main Street

- **in** — 在...里面（三维空间）
  - in the room, in the box, in London, in China

- **on** — 在...上面（接触表面）
  - on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the street

- **under** — 在...正下方
  - under the bed, under the bridge

- **between** — 在两者之间
  - between the post office and the bank

## at / in / on 对比

| 介词 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| at | 点状位置 | at the door, at the corner |
| in | 内部空间 | in the house, in the city |
| on | 表面接触 | on the desk, on the road |

## 常见错误

- ❌ The book is **in** the table. → ✅ The book is **on** the table.
- ❌ I arrived **to** the airport. → ✅ I arrived **at** the airport.
- ❌ She is waiting **on** the bus stop. → ✅ She is waiting **at** the bus stop.
