## 规则

英语中表示时间的最常用介词是 **at, in, on**。

## at — 精确时间点

- at 7:00, at noon, at midnight, at night
- at the moment, at the weekend

## on — 具体某天

- on Monday, on Tuesday morning
- on May 1st, on Christmas Day
- on my birthday, on the weekend (美式英语)

## in — 较长的时间段

- in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
- in May, in summer, in 2024
- in the past, in the future

## 其他常见时间介词

- **for** + 时间段：for two hours, for three years
- **since** + 时间起点：since 2010, since Monday
- **during** + 期间：during the meeting, during summer
- **until** + 终点：until 5pm, until next week

## 常见错误

- ❌ I wake up **in** 7am. → ✅ I wake up **at** 7am.
- ❌ She was born **at** May. → ✅ She was born **in** May.
- ❌ I have been waiting **since** two hours. → ✅ I have been waiting **for** two hours.

## 口诀

**at** 钟点 **on** 天 **in** 月季年
